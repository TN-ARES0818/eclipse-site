01 — Technology

Console Performance.
Mobile Price.

Xframe brings AI-powered frame generation to Android. Mid-range phones locked at 30fps now render at a fluid 60–120fps — without the hardware upgrade. The game renders fewer frames. Xframe fills the rest.

⚡
Frame Interpolation
AI generates intermediate frames in real-time, doubling or quadrupling perceived FPS with minimal latency.
📱
Mid-Range Optimised
Built specifically for Snapdragon 6-series and Dimensity 7000-series chips. No flagship required.
🎮
Universal SDK
Drops into any game engine. Integration under a day. Works with Unity, Unreal, and custom engines.
🔋
Battery Efficient
Rendering fewer native frames means the GPU runs cooler. Xframe reduces battery drain vs native high FPS.

02 — Intelligence

Neura AI —
Built in India.

India-First AI Platform — Building Stage

Neura AI is Axiom's own intelligence platform — fully designed and built in India. Currently in the building stage, Neura is being developed to become India's own AI — not just a product, but a foundation.

Neura Core Building
The central intelligence layer powering Neura AI. Language understanding, reasoning, and response generation — built from the ground up in India.
Perception Engine Active R&D
Real-time scene understanding and object recognition for low-power embedded systems. Optimised for edge deployment.
Neura Assistant Building
An AI assistant that understands India — languages, context, culture. Built natively for Indian users and use cases.
Synthetic Data Lab Research
Generating high-fidelity training data using game engine environments — bridging virtual simulation and physical world accuracy.
